Privacy notice

Updated November 22, 2026

This notice explains how Nook uses information shared through the site submission form.

What you share

The form collects your name and email, information about your site and its potential power capacity, your relationship to the property, and any optional company, phone, or project details you provide.

How we use it

We use your submission to evaluate potential project opportunities, contact you about the site, and manage our project discussions. Submitting the form does not add you to a marketing mailing list.

Storage & access

Submissions are stored in a hosted database and are not displayed publicly. Access is limited to authorized Nook team members and service providers supporting the website and project evaluation. We retain information as needed for evaluating and discussing the opportunity, unless you request its removal or we have a legitimate need to retain it.

Your choices

You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your submission by replying to a Nook representative who contacts you. You may also submit a request through the site form using the same email and including your request and submission reference in the notes field.

Website services

This website uses hosting and database services to operate. Hosting providers may process technical connection information, such as IP address and request logs, for security and reliability. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard connection information when your browser requests them. We have not added advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

Please share only what is needed

Do not submit confidential documents, utility account numbers, personal financial information, or sensitive infrastructure details through this form.
